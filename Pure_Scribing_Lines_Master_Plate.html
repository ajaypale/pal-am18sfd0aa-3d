1. THE 3 FAMILIES OF SCRIBED LINES TRIANGULATION MATRIX

To achieve mathematically perfect 60° equilateral triangular pitch without cumulative layout error, three independent sets of straight lines are scribed across the Prussian Blue dye:

Line FamilyOrientationTool UsedSpacing
Family 1: Rows Horizontal (0°) Vernier Height Gauge ΔY = 7.838 mm steps
Family 2: Rays +60.0° Angle Master Bevel Protractor ΔX = 9.05 mm horizontal pitch
Family 3: Rays 120.0° (-60°) Opposing Set-Square ΔX = 9.05 mm horizontal pitch
The Rule of Three: Every hole center is created by the exact intersection of 1 Horizontal line + 1 (+60°) line + 1 (120°) line. If all 3 lines do not meet at a single microscopic point, the machinist immediately knows a pitch error occurred before any center punch is struck.
